Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

This page covers this website, aubreylubasi.dev, and the two free apps I publish: ZSL Translator and LubaVoice AI. I'm Aubrey Lubasi, and I run all three myself. You can reach me at lubasiaubrey@gmail.com.

This website

  • There are no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or adverts.
  • The fingerprint box on the home page works inside your browser. What you type there is never sent anywhere.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records technical details such as your IP address to run and protect the service. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page opens.
  • If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply. Ask and I'll delete them.

ZSL Translator

  • The app uses your phone's camera to follow hand positions. Video is processed on the phone and is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • The app has no internet permission. It works offline, with no accounts and no analytics.
  • Spoken output uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine.

LubaVoice AI

  • The app uses your microphone to record the sentences you practise. Recordings, your text and your progress are stored on your device only.
  • Files leave the app only when you export them yourself.
  • The app has no internet permission. There are no accounts, no servers and no analytics.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes everything it stored.

Your rights

Under Zambia's Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data I hold about you. Email me and I'll reply. If you're unhappy with my answer, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Changes

If anything here changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
