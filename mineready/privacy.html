MineReady is built and run by Aubrey Lubasi in Zambia. It is at prototype stage and is being tested in a free pilot with a small number of mine contractors. This page explains what data MineReady handles, why, and what you can ask for.

This website

  • There are no forms, cookies, analytics or adverts.

The MineReady app

What it holds. Company documents (such as PACRA, ZRA tax clearance, NAPSA, Workers' Compensation and insurance), worker details (names, job titles and NRC numbers), medical fitness, induction and training certificates, their expiry dates, and photos or scans you upload. The safety module also holds incident reports, pre-shift checks and photos.

Who decides. Your company decides what to upload and is responsible for its workers' data. MineReady stores and processes that data for you, only to track expiry dates, show who is cleared for site and build contractor files.

Where it's kept during the pilot. The pilot app runs on Anthropic's Claude platform. Depending on how you use it, your records are kept either only on your phone, or in a space that only your signed-in account can read. Hosting for the full version hasn't been decided yet. Pilot contractors will be told before that changes.

Who sees it. Your data isn't sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone else. It leaves MineReady only when you send a contractor file or create a share link yourself. The app loads no trackers or analytics.

Photos. Photos are re-encoded on your phone before upload, which removes hidden location data.

Deleting it. You can delete records in the app at any time. Ask me and I'll delete everything held for your company. Records kept only on a phone are deleted when the app's data is cleared.

If something goes wrong. If a security problem affects your data, I'll tell you without delay.

Workers' rights

Under Zambia's Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021, workers whose details are in MineReady can ask to see, correct or delete their data. They can ask their employer or contact me directly. Anyone unhappy with how their data is handled can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Before uploading NRC numbers or medical certificates, please tell your workers that MineReady is used to keep their site documents up to date.

Changes

If anything here changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top, and tell pilot contractors directly.
